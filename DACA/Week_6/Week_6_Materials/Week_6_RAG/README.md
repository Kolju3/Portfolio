## 📚 File Details

### 6_0_R1_viz_data_concepts-rag.md – Data Storytelling Fundamentals

This document covers the **core data storytelling concepts** needed for Week 6 of the DACA programme.

#### Key Topics Covered

| Topic | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Why Storytelling Matters** | Numbers alone don't convince – context and narrative are essential |
| **Story Arc Structure** | Setup → Conflict → Data → Resolution → Action |
| **"So What?" Test** | Every number must answer: why does this matter? |
| **Annotations** | Adding context to charts (text boxes, callouts, arrows) |
| **Reference Lines** | Targets, averages, and benchmarking |
| **Executive Summary** | 3-point structure: Main finding → Evidence → Recommendation |
| **Multi-Audience Design** | CEO vs Marketing vs Operations – same data, different story |
| **Knaflic's Principles** | Beginning → Middle → End; Repetition; Horizontal/Vertical Logic |
| **Career Positioning** | How data storytelling differentiates you in the job market |

#### Example Concepts

**Story Arc Structure:**

| Stage | Question | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Setup** | Who are we? What's the context? | "UrbanStyle is a growing Estonian fashion brand..." |
| **Conflict** | What's the problem? | "...but we didn't know who our best customers are." |
| **Data** | What do the numbers show? | "245 VIP clients generate 42% of revenue." |
| **Resolution** | What do we now know? | "VIP segment is our growth engine." |
| **Action** | What should we do? | "Invest €50K in a loyalty programme." |

**"So What?" Test:**

| Level | Question | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | What's the number? | "Revenue was €305K." |
| 2 | Compared to what? | "That's 23% growth YoY." |
| 3 | What does it mean? | "Q4 holiday season drove growth." |
| 4 | What should we do? | "Start Q4 campaigns 2 months earlier." |

### 6_0_R2_viz_data_urbanstyle_application-rag.md – UrbanStyle Application

This document applies the data storytelling concepts **directly to the UrbanStyle.ltd** business context.

#### Key Topics Covered

| Topic | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Anna's Challenge** | Stakeholders need different stories from the same data |
| **Kristi's Perspective** | Strategic view – growth, risks, sustainability |
| **Liis's Perspective** | Operational view – what's wrong, what to do today |
| **Location-Specific Stories** | Tallinn (growth engine), Tartu (decline to investigate), Pärnu (seasonality), Online (future growth) |
| **Annotations in Practice** | Examples of effective chart annotations |
| **Executive Summary Templates** | Ready-to-use templates for each stakeholder |
| **Cross-Location Comparison** | How to tell a unified story across locations |

#### Example Annotations

| Chart | Annotation | Why It Works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Revenue Trend | "Q4 peak: Christmas campaign +35%" | Explains the spike |
| Revenue Trend | "Summer dip: seasonal pattern" | Normalises the decline |
| Top Products | "HERO product: Denim Jacket – 28% of revenue" | Highlights importance |
| City Distribution | "Tartu -5% – operational audit needed" | Shows awareness of risk |

#### Location-Specific Narrative Examples

**Tallinn (Growth Engine):**
> "Tallinn is UrbanStyle's largest store, generating 37.5% of total revenue. Revenue grew 18% YoY, driven by Facebook campaigns and the Denim Jacket product line. We recommend increasing marketing investment in the capital and expanding the Denim range."

**Tartu (Decline to Investigate):**
> "Tartu shows a -5% decline YoY. Online sales in the Tartu region are growing, suggesting potential cannibalisation. We recommend an operational audit to identify whether the issue is stock, staff, or customer experience."

**Pärnu (Seasonality):**
> "Pärnu is a seasonal store, with 60% of annual revenue coming from the summer tourist season (June-August). Winter months are significantly below average. We recommend a winter strategy to smooth seasonality and increase off-season revenue."

**Online (Future Growth):**
> "Online is UrbanStyle's fastest-growing channel at 25% YoY growth. It has lower customer acquisition costs and wider geographic reach. We recommend increasing digital investment and optimising the e-commerce experience."
