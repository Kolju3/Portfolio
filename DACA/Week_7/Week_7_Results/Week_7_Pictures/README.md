## 📌 Overview

This folder contains the **visualisations** generated during Week 7 to illustrate the RFM segmentation results. All plots were created using Python (Plotly Express) and are interactive in the notebook.

---

## 📸 Plot Gallery

| Filename | Description | Key Insight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `klientide_jaotus.png` | Bar chart of customer distribution across segments | Potential segment is largest (29.88%), VIP Champions 17.91% |
| `recency_monetary_scatter.png` | Scatter plot: Recency (days) vs Monetary (€), log scale, coloured by segment | Clear clusters: VIP (low recency, high monetary), Lost (high recency, low monetary) |
| `top10_vip_donut.png` | Donut chart showing Top 10 VIP Champions' revenue share | Top 10 VIPs account for 8.64% of total RFM revenue (€2.677M) |
| `segment_monetary_share.png` | Bar chart showing monetary share by segment | VIP + Loyal = 72.57% of total monetary value |
