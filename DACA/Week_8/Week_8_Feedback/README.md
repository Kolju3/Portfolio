## 🔍 Key Learnings

### 1. Python is Powerful for Data Analysis

> *"Pandas makes it so easy to clean and transform data. I could never do this in Excel."*

**What I learned:**
- `pandas` is much more efficient than Excel for large datasets
- Chaining methods makes code clean and readable
- Jupyter notebooks are perfect for exploratory analysis

### 2. Data Quality is Everything

> *"Garbage in = garbage out. All the analysis in the world won't help if the data is wrong."*

**What I learned:**
- Always start with data quality checks
- Handle NULLs and duplicates before analysis
- Document your cleaning steps

### 3. Visualisation Tells the Story

> *"A good chart is worth a thousand numbers. The dashboard makes the data come alive."*

**What I learned:**
- Choose the right chart for the data
- Keep it simple – don't overcomplicate
- Annotate key insights

### 4. Business Context Matters

> *"Understanding the business question is more important than knowing the technical solution."*

**What I learned:**
- Always start with the business question
- Connect your analysis back to business impact
- Speak the language of your stakeholders

## 💡 Lessons for Next Week

### What to Continue

1. **Collaborate early** – share ideas before the group session
2. **Document everything** – clear READMEs save time later
3. **Test your code** – don't wait until the last minute
4. **Ask questions** – if you're stuck, speak up

### What to Improve

1. **Start earlier** – don't leave the analysis to the last day
2. **More practice** – Python skills need regular practice
3. **Business focus** – always connect analysis to business impact
4. **Storytelling** – practice presenting insights
