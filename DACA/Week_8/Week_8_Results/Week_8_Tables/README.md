## 🔍 Key Insights Summary

### 1. Customer Concentration

- **10%** of customers generate **60%** of revenue
- High-value customers have **€571** AOV vs. €95 for medium-value

### 2. Product Focus

- **Women's Dresses** = highest revenue and margin
- **Accessories** = highest margin (62%)
- **15 slow-moving products** – consider clearance or removal

### 3. Channel Strategy

- **Online** = 60% revenue, growing 15% faster than in-store
- **Store** = higher AOV (€165 vs. €112)

### 4. Marketing Efficiency

- **Email** = 400% ROI, €5 CAC
- **Instagram** = highest absolute revenue
- **Facebook** = lowest ROI (100%)
