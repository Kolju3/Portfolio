3. **Present key insights** with actionable recommendations

## 🔍 Key Findings Presented

### 1. Customer Analysis (Natalia)

| Finding | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Customer Concentration** | 10% of customers generate 60% of revenue |
| **High-Value AOV** | €571 vs. €95 for medium-value customers |
| **Repeat Purchase Rate** | 45% of customers make repeat purchases |

**Recommendation:** Focus retention efforts on high-value customers.

### 2. Product Analysis (Natalia)

| Finding | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Top Category** | Women's Dresses – €450k revenue, 58% margin |
| **Highest Margin** | Accessories – 62% margin |
| **Slow-Moving Products** | 15 products with <5 sales in 2024 |

**Recommendation:** Expand women's dress collection, promote accessories.

### 3. Channel Analysis (Olga)

| Finding | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Online Revenue** | 60% of total, growing 15% faster than in-store |
| **Store AOV** | €165 vs. €112 online |
| **Missing Location Data** | Now resolved |

**Recommendation:** Invest in online experience while maintaining store quality.

### 4. Marketing Analysis (Kalju)

| Finding | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Highest ROI** | Email marketing – 400% ROI |
| **Highest Revenue** | Instagram – €30k revenue from €10k spend |
| **Lowest ROI** | Facebook – 100% ROI |

**Recommendation:** Shift budget from Facebook to Email and Instagram.
