## 📌 Overview

This folder contains the materials and notes for the **final presentation** to the UrbanStyle board of directors. The presentation was delivered during Sessioon 3 (Demo session) and followed a strict **7‑minute** format.

The goal was to demonstrate the **business value** of our work, not just the technical details.

---

## 📊 Presentation Structure (7 Minutes)

| Part | Duration | Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Introduction** | 0:30 | Team introduction and our “data story” |
| **2. Relay – Individual Strengths** | ~3:00 | Each member presents one key project (45 sec each) |
| **3. Synthesis** | 1:00 | Summary: “What UrbanStyle learned from our work” |
| **4. Recommendations** | 1:00 | Three data‑driven recommendations for the board |
| **5. AI Usage** | 1:00 | How we used AI throughout the programme |
| **6. Board Thanks** | 0:30 | Mentor as Kristi Tamm thanks the team |

Total: **7 minutes**

---

## 🎯 My 45‑Second Relay Script

> “I am Kalju. My strongest project is city name standardisation – UrbanStyle had inconsistent city data that made accurate customer segmentation impossible. I wrote SQL queries to identify and clean these inconsistencies. The result: we now have accurate city‑level customer counts, which means the marketing team can target campaigns precisely. This is an example of how data quality directly impacts business decisions.”
