## 📌 Overview

This folder contains the **course materials** provided for Week 10 of the DACA programme. The focus is on **portfolio defense** – how to present and justify your work to stakeholders.

The materials provide guidance on:
- Structuring a professional portfolio (GitHub READMEs, project organisation)
- Preparing a 7‑minute board presentation
- Answering common interview and presentation questions
- Reflecting on the Shu‑Ha‑Ri learning journey
