## 📌 Overview

This folder contains the **results, outputs, and deliverables** from Week 9 of the DACA programme. Unlike previous weeks (which involved SQL queries, Python analysis, or visualizations), Week 9 focused on **career preparation assets** – CV, LinkedIn profile, interview materials, and the group hiring guide.

The outputs are organised into four subfolders:

| Subfolder | Contents |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Week_9_Conclusions/** | Summary of the week's outcomes and key findings |
| **Week_9_Pictures/** | Screenshots (LinkedIn profile, CV examples) |
| **Week_9_Presentation/** | Group presentation materials (PDF) |
| **Week_9_Tables/** | Structured tables (CV structure, STAR answers, LinkedIn formula) |
