## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. CV is a Marketing Document

- **Quantification** is the most important thing – every bullet point must contain numbers
- **ATS-friendly** – 70% of large companies use machine filtering
- **Action verbs** – every sentence starts with a verb (Analyzed, Built, Designed)

### 2. STAR Method Makes Interview Answers Powerful

| Part | Example |
| :--- | :--- |
| **S** - Situation | "UrbanStyle had 10,000+ sales records with duplicates and missing values." |
| **T** - Task | "My task was to clean the data and identify high-value customers." |
| **A** - Action | "I used SQL to remove duplicates and Python to calculate RFM scores." |
| **R** - Result | "Identified 424 VIP customers worth €1,111,362 annually." |

### 3. LinkedIn is Your 24/7 Recruiter Magnet

- **Headline formula:** `[Role] | [Key Skills] | [Value Proposition]`
- **About:** 3 paragraphs (who you are, what you do, what you offer)
- **Featured:** GitHub, dashboard screenshot, DACA completion post

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

1. **CV = marketing document** – numbers and results matter most
2. **STAR method** – structure answers so interviewers remember your results
3. **LinkedIn** – optimized profile works for you 24/7
4. **Career changer status is an advantage** – previous experience + new data skills = unique combination
5. **You are the product** – CV, LinkedIn, and interview skills must work together
