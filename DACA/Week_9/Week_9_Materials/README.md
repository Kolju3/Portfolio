## 📌 Overview

This folder contains all the **course materials** provided during Week 9 of the DACA programme. The focus of this week was **Career Preparation** – CV writing, interview preparation, LinkedIn optimization, and portfolio presentation.

The materials are divided into two main subfolders:
- **`N9_0_X_P_*` files** – Workbooks and reference materials
- **`Week_9_RAG/`** – RAG-optimised background documents for NotebookLM
