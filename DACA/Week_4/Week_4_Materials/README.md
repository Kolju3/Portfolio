## 📌 Overview

This folder contains all the **course materials** provided during Week 4 of the DACA programme. The focus of this week is **SQL Aggregation** – learning how to use `GROUP BY`, `HAVING`, CTEs, and window functions to transform raw data into business intelligence.

The materials are divided into two main subfolders:
- **Main materials** – The workbook and group work guide
- **`Week_4_RAG/`** – RAG-optimised background documents for NotebookLM
