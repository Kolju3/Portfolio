## 📌 Overview

This document captures my personal reflections on Week 1 of the DACA programme. It describes the group work experience, the challenges we faced as a team, and the lessons learned about collaboration.

---

## 👥 Team Collaboration

### The Confusion

There was **some confusion** at the start of the week. Nobody in the team knew exactly what to do. The tasks were new, the tools were unfamiliar, and the expectations were not entirely clear.

> *"Keegi täpselt ei teadnud mida teha."*
> – *"Nobody exactly knew what to do."*

### How We Managed

Despite the initial confusion, we managed to **organise ourselves** and complete the work.

The main issue we encountered was that because nobody knew anything at that point, there was a strong need to **research and work individually**. This created a dynamic where:

- People felt they needed to figure things out on their own before contributing
- There was a reluctance to "waste" group time on individual learning
- Collaboration felt forced when everyone was still in the learning phase

> *"Valdavalt üksik töö – selle tõttu ei tahetud gruppi ajal väga teha."*
> – *"Predominantly individual work – because of this, there was not much desire to work in the group during group time."*

---

## 🔍 Specific Observations

| Challenge | Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lack of clarity** | No one fully understood the tasks at the beginning |
| **Individual learning** | Everyone needed to research and learn independently first |
| **Reluctance to collaborate** | Group time felt inefficient when everyone was still figuring things out |
| **No clear direction** | There was no single "expert" to guide the team |

---

## ✅ What Went Well

| Area | Experience |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Self-organisation** | Despite the confusion, we eventually managed to organise ourselves |
| **Task completion** | All required work was completed successfully |
| **Individual effort** | Everyone contributed and did their part |
| **Persistence** | We didn't give up despite the initial chaos |

---

## 💡 Key Learnings

1. **Group work requires a shared understanding** – it's difficult to collaborate effectively when everyone is at different levels of understanding
2. **Individual preparation is essential** – group time should be used for synthesis, not for basic learning
3. **Communication is key** – stating "I don't know" or "I'm confused" helps the team adjust expectations
4. **It's okay to be confused** – this is normal at the start of a new programme

---

## 📝 Future Improvements

- Start individual research earlier in the week
- Use group time for structured collaboration (e.g., pair programming, review)
- Be more explicit about what each team member needs to prepare before group sessions
- Establish a clearer "first step" for the group to get started
